## 2. Gap analysis

### Why it slid in October (my view)

1. **The "58 to 89" is mostly a sampling artefact; the real move is smaller and started in September.** The head term sat at 68-76 from launch through September. It never ranked well. Weekly impressions then collapsed from 241 (week of Aug 31) to 7 (week of Oct 5), and the two "good" weeks (58.1 and 34.3) are averages over 27 and 7 impressions of long-tail queries. When the page came back into results from Oct 19 it came back lower: head term 74.7 → 87.3 → 90.6 → 93.2. So the real change is about 15-20 places on the head term plus roughly 60% fewer weekly impressions than early September.
2. **Timing points at the September core update, delayed.** Impressions fell from the week of Sep 14, inside the Sep 12-26 core update (spam update from Sep 24). The 2026-10-01 memory note said alternatives pages "held", but that was read before this page's impressions had finished falling. The only edit since publish is the Sep 22 Salesforce link (`scripts/link-salesforce.mjs`), which landed after the decline began.
3. **It looks cluster-level, not page-level.** hubspot-alternatives fell to 62 impressions at 58.7 the week of Nov 2 and salesforce-cost slid in October, all with no edits. Three CRM-comparison pages moving together suggests Google re-scored the site's commercial CRM cluster. A DR 62 site at position 90 for a KD 0-1 keyword is not an authority problem.
4. **Competitively, our edge expired.** The August angle was "competitors cite Pipedrive's dead tiers". Clientify's list (#4, dated August 2026) now quotes Lite $14 / Growth $39 / Premium $59 / Ultimate $79 "checked on 24 August 2026", with a cost-per-year section, nine tool sections, a migration section and a when-to-stay section, in 4,245 words. Zapier and LeadsBridge quote no Pipedrive prices at all. So our two openers ("nearly every list is written by a CRM company and ranks itself first", "most articles still cite its old tiers") are no longer true, and the page is the thinnest list on page one.
5. **Technical cause ruled out as far as I can check:** live URL returns 200, `index, follow`, self-canonical, present in the sitemap, 1 H1, FAQPage + Article + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD. Not checked: GSC URL Inspection (not in the API). Worth one manual look, as for the pitchbook page.

Implication: on-page fixes are necessary (wrong facts, thin page) but may not be sufficient. Read this cycle together with the hubspot-alternatives cycle applied 2026-11-12. If neither moves by the review, stop editing content on this cluster and look at site-level causes.
