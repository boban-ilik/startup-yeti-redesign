### Fix now: claims on the live page that are now wrong

1. **"Build, Grow, and Scale all advertise 'unlimited stakeholders'."** Carta's live plans page (checked 2026-11-12) says Build is "Up to 50 stakeholders", and Grow and Scale have a "Flexible stakeholder limit". 2. **Pulley is recommended as the first alternative (body + FAQ).** Pulley's pricing page banner and its own shutdown FAQ (published Sept 15, 2026) say Pulley ceases operations December 8, 2026 and has an exclusive migration partnership with Carta. AngelList (same bullet) now lists only fund administration and investor management on its equity-management page, and Vela could not be verified. Recommending a shutting-down product on a pricing page is a trust problem for readers and for AI Overview citation.
