# Startup Yeti editorial profile

- **Represents:** Startup Yeti (https://www.startupyeti.com), a publication. Articles are bylined to Boban Ilik (About page https://www.startupyeti.com/about) but written in the editorial "we". No first-hand experience is claimed unless Boban supplies it for a specific article.
- **Audience:** pre-seed to Series B SaaS founders and early operators. They know MRR, churn, CAC and gross margin loosely, have heard the popular rules of thumb, and need a number they can defend in a board deck or spend decision.
- **Positioning:** the "real number" source. SaaS metrics, pricing and growth pages that reconcile primary data and correct popular rules of thumb instead of repeating them. Commercial interests: none in the topics covered; the site's own free calculator is the only owned product linked.
- **Goal:** rank for founder metric and pricing queries and send readers to the next useful step, usually the calculator.
- **Call to action:** the next useful step only (calculator when its metric is covered, otherwise a related guide). No sales pitch, no newsletter push.
- **Intake defaults (owner preferences, 2026-11-20):** United States, English; automatic approval mode inside the skill, with the owner's own gates outside it (draft approval, then image approval, before publishing); request founder forum and Reddit question research for every new article, inside the skill's shared USD 0.50 research allowance, with no budget increase without asking; native writer and editor routes.
- **Out of scope for the skill:** publishing, CMS edits, images and incoming links are done afterwards by the repo scripts (`scripts/md-to-article.mjs`, `scripts/publish.mjs`).
